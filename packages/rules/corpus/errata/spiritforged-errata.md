Hey Riftbounders!

We’ve got some fresh card errata for you today, covering cards from both Origins and Spiritforged. If you’re just starting Riftbound, or new to TCGs, card errata refers to any updates to card text after a card was printed. We do this to make cards more easily understood and to clarify how they work within the rules of the game.

You can find the card errata in the above PDF, or in the text below. If you’re hungry for a deep dive into our philosophy about errata or why these changes were made, check out our [Riftbound Spiritforged FAQ](https://riftbound.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/rules-and-releases/riftbound-spiritforged-faq), which goes into greater detail about these changes.
